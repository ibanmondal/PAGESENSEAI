How to Install

1
Download & Extract: Click the download button above and extract the downloaded pagesense-extension.zip file to a folder on your computer.
2
Open Extensions: In Google Chrome, go to chrome://extensions/ in your address bar.
3
Developer Mode: Toggle "Developer mode" ON in the top right corner.
4
Load Unpacked: Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top left and select the folder where you extracted the extension.
